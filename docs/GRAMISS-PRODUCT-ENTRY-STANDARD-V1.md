# GRAMISS PRODUCT ENTRY STANDARD V1

Status: Active source of truth for new WooCommerce products
Scope: New product creation and publish readiness on `gramiss.ir`
Established from: GRAMISS SEO BIBLE + live Product Entry Readiness Audit V1 + Product Indexability Diagnosis V1

## Objective

Every newly published product must enter the catalogue with complete commercial data, a stable URL, correct WooCommerce relationships, useful product copy, a deliberate indexation decision, one valid Product schema owner, and enough structured information to be safely reused by search, feeds, filters, recommendations, and future AI features.

This standard applies to **new products going forward**. Existing catalogue debt is tracked separately and must not be bulk-corrected by guessing.

## Current live baseline that shaped this standard

At the time this contract was established:

- 48 products were published and 8 were drafts.
- 45 of the 48 published products were variable products; 3 were simple products.
- 149 sellable variations existed across published products.
- all 48 published product pages returned HTTP 200.
- all 48 rendered exactly one Product schema object; no duplicate Product schema was detected.
- published product image-alt gaps had already been reduced to zero.
- all published products had a full product description and a featured image.
- 47 of 48 published products lacked a short description/excerpt.
- all 48 published products lacked a parent/master SKU.
- 10 published variations lacked a SKU.
- 2 published variations lacked a price.
- 5 variations were out of stock.
- Rank Math global product title template is `خرید %title% %sep% %sitename%`.
- Rank Math global product meta-description template is `%excerpt%`.
- public product pages can therefore have valid rendered metadata without per-product Rank Math overrides, but the short description is operationally important because it feeds the global meta-description template.
- 2 published products (IDs 62 and 68) have explicit `noindex, follow`, no rendered canonical, and are excluded from Product Sitemap; this is why indexability must become an explicit publish decision rather than an accidental legacy state.
- Product Sitemap contains 47 URLs because it contains 45 indexable products plus `/shop/`; it intentionally excludes the two noindex products above.

## 1. Product identity contract

Before publish, every product must have:

- a verified, human-readable product name;
- one stable product ID generated by WordPress/WooCommerce;
- one stable slug based on the verified product identity;
- the correct WooCommerce product type (`simple`, `variable`, or another deliberately chosen type);
- a clear distinction from any already-published product with a confusingly similar name;
- no unsupported brand, material, model, origin, performance, or authenticity claims.

### URL rule

The canonical URL format is:

`/product/<stable-product-slug>/`

Do not casually rename a published product slug after indexing. Any later URL change requires:

1. explicit old -> new URL mapping;
2. permanent redirect;
3. self-canonical verification on the new URL;
4. sitemap verification;
5. internal-link verification.

## 2. Taxonomy contract

Every publishable product must have:

- at least one valid `product_cat` category;
- one primary category that best represents the product;
- secondary categories only when they describe a real, useful navigation or merchandising relationship;
- global WooCommerce attributes for reusable dimensions such as color and size when applicable.

Do not create a new category merely to describe one isolated product unless there is a real catalogue/navigation need.

Do not auto-correct ambiguous historical taxonomy assignments. Product 355 and category 217 remain examples of why taxonomy changes require verified product intent.

## 3. SKU contract

### Parent/master SKU

Every new product must receive a unique parent/master SKU from the merchant's authoritative SKU convention before publish.

The standard requires uniqueness and presence; it does **not** invent the merchant's numbering format.

### Variation SKU

Every sellable variation must have a unique SKU before publish.

A variable product fails the publish gate if any active/sellable variation lacks a SKU.

Never synthesize a missing SKU for an existing or new product unless the authoritative SKU convention/source is available.

## 4. Price and stock contract

Every sellable variation must have an authoritative price before publish.

A variable product fails the publish gate when any active variation has no effective price.

The system must never infer a missing price from a sibling variation.

Every product/variation must also have a valid stock status. Quantity is required only when stock management for that item is enabled.

`outofstock` is a valid deliberate state; a missing/unknown stock configuration is not.

## 5. Variation contract

For variable products:

- variation-defining attributes must be marked for variation use;
- every child variation must map to legitimate attribute values;
- each sellable variation must have a unique SKU;
- each sellable variation must have a price;
- stock status must be explicit;
- duplicated or unreachable attribute combinations are not allowed;
- variation data must remain native WooCommerce data so cart/checkout behavior is preserved.

## 6. Media contract

Before publish:

- featured image is required;
- gallery images are added when they provide a useful additional product view;
- every product image used on the product has non-empty, useful alt text;
- alt text describes the actual visible product/view and must not be keyword-stuffed;
- image alt text must not invent unseen material, color, brand, model, or technical properties.

The live catalogue currently has zero published empty-alt image gaps; this must remain the floor, not regress.

## 7. Product copy contract

### Full description — required

The main product description must contain only verified product facts and useful purchase information. Depending on what is actually known, this may include:

- fit/cut;
- visible design details;
- verified fabric/material composition;
- verified color;
- available size/variation information;
- care guidance only when sourced from the actual product/label/manufacturer;
- practical styling or usage context when it does not turn into unsupported claims.

Do not use generic filler or fabricate specifications simply to make a description longer.

### Short description / excerpt — required

Every new product must have a concise, product-specific short description before publish.

This is a publish blocker because Rank Math currently renders the product meta description from `%excerpt%`.

A good short description should:

- identify the actual product;
- mention only verified differentiators;
- be readable as a search-result description;
- avoid fake urgency and unsupported superlatives;
- avoid duplicating the full description verbatim.

## 8. SEO metadata contract

### SEO title

Per-product `rank_math_title` is **not mandatory** when the global product template produces a clear, unique rendered title.

Current global fallback:

`خرید %title% %sep% %sitename%`

Use a per-product title only when there is a deliberate SEO reason and the override remains truthful to the product.

### Meta description

Per-product `rank_math_description` is **not mandatory** when the product short description produces a useful rendered description through `%excerpt%`.

The gate validates the **public rendered description**, not merely whether a database override exists.

### Canonical

Do not require a stored `rank_math_canonical_url` for ordinary products.

The requirement is the public result:

- exactly one canonical link;
- it resolves to the product's own stable URL.

Explicit canonical overrides are reserved for genuine exceptions.

## 9. Indexation contract

Indexability is a deliberate product-level decision.

### Default for a valid public sellable product

`index, follow`

### `noindex, follow` is allowed only when intentional

If a published product is intentionally noindex, the reason must be documented. Examples could include a temporary catalogue state or a deliberate duplicate/utility use case, but the reason must exist before publish.

A noindex product is expected to be excluded from Product Sitemap.

The current IDs 62 and 68 are legacy examples of published noindex products. They are **not** to be silently changed by this standard; they belong to the remediation review queue.

## 10. Sitemap contract

For a published indexable product:

- the product URL must appear in Product Sitemap;
- the URL in the sitemap must equal the public canonical product URL;
- no old query-style or duplicate URL should appear as a second indexable product URL.

For a deliberate noindex product:

- exclusion from Product Sitemap is expected.

Do not assume Product Sitemap count equals published-product count because Rank Math may also include the Shop landing page. Validate membership by URL, not raw count alone.

## 11. Schema ownership contract

A normal product page must render exactly **one** Product schema owner.

Current production successfully renders one Product schema object per published product. Do not add a second custom Product JSON-LD layer on top of WooCommerce/Rank Math.

The public gate must verify:

- Product schema exists;
- Product schema count is exactly one;
- article-only schema is not introduced as a competing primary schema;
- schema values come from authoritative WooCommerce/product data.

## 12. Internal linking contract

New products receive their primary structural internal links through native WooCommerce taxonomy, shop/category listings, breadcrumbs, and related-product systems.

Editorial links are contextual, not mandatory on every product.

Add a content-to-product/category link only when the article's search intent genuinely helps the buyer make that decision. Do not create forced sitewide keyword links.

## 13. Publish gate — blocking requirements

A new product must **not** be published when any of the following is true:

- product identity/title is unresolved;
- slug is missing or knowingly unstable;
- product type is wrong/uncertain;
- no valid product category is assigned;
- parent/master SKU is missing;
- a sellable variation has no SKU;
- a sellable variation has no price;
- stock status is invalid/undefined;
- required variation attributes are incomplete;
- featured image is missing;
- any product image used by the product has empty alt text;
- full description is empty;
- short description/excerpt is empty;
- index/noindex decision is not explicit;
- the product would make an unsupported commercial/material/brand claim.

## 14. Post-publish QA — required

For a product intended to be indexable, verify after publish:

1. product page returns HTTP 200;
2. stable pretty URL is used;
3. rendered SEO title is non-empty and product-specific;
4. rendered meta description is non-empty and product-specific;
5. robots contains `index` and does not contain `noindex`;
6. canonical is self-referencing;
7. exactly one Product schema is rendered;
8. Product Sitemap contains the canonical URL;
9. primary category resolves and is internally connected;
10. protected Home/Looks implementation is unchanged by product-entry automation.

For an intentional noindex product, QA changes to:

- HTTP 200;
- documented noindex reason;
- `noindex, follow` rendered;
- excluded from Product Sitemap;
- Product schema remains valid for users/consumers where appropriate;
- canonical handling reviewed explicitly rather than assumed.

## 15. Automation boundary

### Safe to automate/read-only gate

- presence/uniqueness checks;
- category/attribute validation;
- missing SKU detection;
- missing price detection;
- stock-state validation;
- image/alt checks;
- description/excerpt checks;
- rendered title/description/canonical/robots QA;
- sitemap membership QA;
- Product schema count QA;
- duplicate-title warning;
- taxonomy anomaly warning.

### Do not auto-invent or auto-correct

- product price;
- stock quantity/status without source data;
- parent or variation SKU values without the authoritative SKU convention;
- material composition;
- brand/authenticity claims;
- category changes when product intent is ambiguous;
- product-name corrections that might change the actual commercial identity;
- existing live URL changes without redirects and a migration plan.

## 16. Legacy remediation queue — separate from new-product intake

The following existing issues are **not** permission to bulk-edit the live catalogue:

- products 62 and 68: published but explicit `noindex, follow`; review the business/indexation intent before changing;
- products 49, 97 and 141: published variation-SKU gaps remain;
- products 210 and 344: at least one published variation has no price; authoritative price required before correction;
- all 48 current published parents lack a parent/master SKU under the present dataset;
- 47 of 48 current published products lack a short description/excerpt;
- historical taxonomy/name review candidates, including product 355 and category 217, require product-level verification.

Handle these as isolated remediation batches with a dry-run report and rollback path. Do not let legacy debt weaken the rules for new products.

## 17. Definition of done for a new Gramiss product

A new product is SEO/catalogue-ready only when:

- commercial identity is verified;
- WooCommerce type/taxonomy/attributes are correct;
- parent SKU exists;
- all sellable variations have unique SKU + authoritative price + stock state;
- media and alt text are complete;
- full + short descriptions contain verified information;
- public title/description render correctly;
- indexation decision is explicit;
- an indexable product renders self-canonical and is in Product Sitemap;
- exactly one Product schema is present;
- no protected Home/Looks assets were changed by the process.

This contract is the required baseline for all product-entry tooling built after V1.
